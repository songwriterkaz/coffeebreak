Free hair days Free

Volunteer hairdressers and salons give free haircuts and perms to NDIS participants. There is no cost to participants and you never need to pay or use your plan funds. Support workers are welcome to come along.

Perms take longer, often two to three hours, and the hairdresser may do a quick strand test first. Tell us about any access needs when you book, such as quiet time, wheelchair access, sensory needs or a short appointment, and we will do our best to match you with the right salon.

New hair days are being organised. You can still book a spot now and tell us your preferred day, and we will contact you when a day is set.

About Coffeebreak Hair Day

Coffeebreak Hair Day is a community service organised by Karen O'Brien, a Coffeebreak songwriter and creator from Newcastle NSW. Local hairdressers, salons and hairdressing students donate their time so NDIS participants can get a fresh cut or a perm for free, in a friendly and accessible setting.

It is free for participants. No one will ever ask a participant for payment or plan details.
