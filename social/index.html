Community rules
  • Be kind. No bullying, harassment, hate or threats.
  • Only post things you made or have the right to share. No copied music, films or other people's work.
  • No nudity, sexual content, violence or anything illegal. No posting other people's private details.
  • You must be 16 or older to join.
  • Use Report on any post that breaks the rules. Accounts that break the rules can be suspended.
Privacy

Your username, name, bio, picture and posts are public to everyone who visits. Private messages can only be seen by you and the person you message. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed) and are never readable. You can delete your account at any time from Me, and that removes your profile, posts, uploads and messages.
