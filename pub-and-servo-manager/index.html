How to play

Each day runs from 6:00 to midnight. Cars pull up to the bowsers, buy fuel and often grab a pie, a coffee or a cold drink from the shop. The pub opens at 11:00 for lunch and gets busy after work.

Keep tanks and shelves stocked by ordering from suppliers, set fair prices, hire staff so queues stay short, and buy upgrades to grow. Prices that are too high, empty shelves and long queues hurt your reputation. Wages and running costs are paid at close.
