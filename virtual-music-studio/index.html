Drum Kit

Hit the pads or press 1 to 8. Build a beat in the step sequencer and press Play beat.

Pink Electric Guitar

Strum a chord (keys Z X C V B N M and comma) or pick single notes on the neck. It plays through the amp.

Pink Bass Guitar

Four strings, five frets. Pick a tone and lay down a groove.

Studio Mic

Turn on your mic, sing or talk, and record it on a track. Wear headphones when you listen back so it does not squeal.

Backing tracks by Coffeebreak

Sing along to a Coffeebreak song. The song plays on the Backing channel in the mixer.

Amps and Effects

The amp shapes the guitar and bass. Delay and reverb go across the whole studio.

Mixer

Multitrack Recorder
