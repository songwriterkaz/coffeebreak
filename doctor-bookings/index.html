About Coffeebreak Doctor Bookings

An appointment booker for medical clinics that runs entirely in your browser: live availability, instant booking, automatic next-available slots, a waitlist that fills cancellations, reminders with calendar files, telehealth, new and returning patient forms, a doctor dashboard with check-in and no-show tracking, multi-clinic search and a Personal Reception chat assistant.
