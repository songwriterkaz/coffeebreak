Parent setup

This is a helper, not a tracking service. A web page cannot follow a child once it is closed, and it cannot watch where a child is in the background. Location is read only when your child taps the location button, and it goes nowhere unless a text is sent from your child's messaging app. Everything below is saved only on this device. Teach your child to go to a police officer or a shop worker, and that in Australia the number to call in an emergency is 000.

Other places (Nan's, school and so on)

Privacy on the kid screen

The PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as the number. It stops casual snooping only. Saved details are not encrypted, so keep the phone locked too.

Notes for parents

  • Open this page once with internet, then use Add to Home Screen. After that it works offline. Calls, texts and maps still need phone signal.
  • Send my location: your child taps the button, allows location in the browser, then taps Text to Mum or Dad and presses Send in the messages app. Nothing goes out without those taps.
  • Keep sharing while I walk only updates while this page stays open and the screen stays on. A web page cannot track a child after it closes, so this is not a replacement for a real tracking device or a family locator app.
  • If location is turned off or blocked, the 000, Mum, Dad and police station buttons still work.
  • Walking directions and Find a police station open Google Maps or Apple Maps in your child's browser. That maps service then sees the destination, as it would for any search.
  • Practise with your child: press the buttons together, and talk about police officers, shop workers and staying put.
  • If a child is lost right now, call 000.
