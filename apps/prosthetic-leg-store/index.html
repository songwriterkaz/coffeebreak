How it works

Pick any design and a layout: Feature (one big design), Tiled (the design repeated in flash cards) or Pink Fishnet (the design framed on a pink fishnet stocking with a lace top and bow). Then choose a printable wrap download, a print-ready 30 x 40 cm file at 150 dpi with cut and bleed lines, emailed once payment arrives, or a made-to-order printed cover sized to your measurements.

Covers are decorative, not functional parts of a prosthesis. Please check with your prosthetist before fitting anything over your socket or components.

How to measure

  1. Measure with the leg fitted or standing upright, using a soft tape measure.
  2. Length: from the bottom of the socket rim (or where you want the cover to start) down to the top of the foot or ankle.
  3. Top circumference: around the widest part at the top, just below the socket rim.
  4. Middle circumference: halfway down.
  5. Bottom circumference: just above the foot or ankle.
  6. Tell us the leg type (below knee or above knee), left or right, and any parts that must stay uncovered such as valves, buttons or the knee joint.
  7. Add 1 to 2 cm overlap to each circumference if you want the wrap to join with an overlap.

Put all measurements in the notes box at checkout, in centimetres. The same numbers go straight into the Leg Maker.
