Coffeebreak 3D Dental Print

Prepare a model for a dental printer. Education and visualisation only.

Full version US$10 for customers.
Not a medical device. Not TGA-registered. Not for diagnosis or treatment planning.

This is an education, visualisation and print-preparation tool. Any appliance or guide used in a patient must be designed and validated by a registered dental practitioner using approved software and biocompatible resins under the manufacturer's instructions.

Do not store patient data here. Remove patient names from files before you import them. Projects stay in this browser and are not uploaded.
