Coffeebreak Park Builder
Open index.html in a browser (or play online). Walk with WASD or the joystick, jump with Space or Jump.
Build, Rotate, Place, Delete, Music, Outfit, My Park and Done are at the bottom.
Your park saves on this device automatically.
Play live: real players in the same room see each other, and Players lets you visit their parks. Only real players are shown, and there is no chat.
Private room for friends: add ?room=yourname to the game link.
Made by Coffeebreak.
