Title: Coffeebreak Tube
Short description: Share and watch videos with profiles where you choose your own gender and pronouns. Comments off by default.
Tags: video, video-player, lgbtq, pronouns, inclusive, social, pwa, offline, creator, coffeebreak

Description:
Coffeebreak Tube is a video app from Coffeebreak. Upload and play your own videos, build a channel, search, like, make playlists and save videos to watch later.

Every person picks their own gender and pronouns, from a wide list or in their own words, and decides whether to show them. Pronouns appear next to names on videos, channels and search. The app never guesses anyone's gender.

Comments are off by default. Turn them on per video if you want them.

Local demo: everything is saved on your device until a free backend is connected. Use Export to back up your channel and Import to bring it back.
