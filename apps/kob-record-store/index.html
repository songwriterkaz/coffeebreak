About

This is the Coffeebreak country counter. The songs on the shelves are titles already released under Coffeebreak, including the radio set and the records-store albums. Digital copies are US$10. Physical formats are pre-orders at US$10. You are buying from Coffeebreak / KOB.

How it works

  1. Search the shelves. Filter by Digital or Physical, and by format.
  2. Add a title to your wishlist or your cart. Both stay in this browser.
  3. At checkout, pay by PayID (Teachers Mutual Bank): darkokaz@gmx.com. Put the reference in the payment description.
  4. This page does not take card payments and it does not mark an order paid by itself.
