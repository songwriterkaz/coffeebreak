SIM-MOM 3D · Robotic Birthing Manikin

You are the midwife. Guide Emma (29, G2 P1, 39+4 weeks) through labour and birth, from admission to the first hour after birth. Choose your actions, time her pushing with contractions, and watch the robotic fetus move through the pelvis. Orbit the 3D manikin (drag / scroll) and click the glowing hotspots to examine her.

Disclaimer: This is an educational simulation for students and clinicians in training. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for accredited training, supervised clinical practice or local guidelines (e.g. RANZCOG, ANZCOR, state health policies). Clinical details are simplified and the timeline is compressed.
