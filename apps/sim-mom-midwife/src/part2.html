Virtual Birthing Manikin

You are the midwife. Guide Emma (29, G2 P1, 39+4 weeks) through labour and birth, from admission to the first hour after birth. Choose your actions, time her pushing with contractions, and watch the fetus move through the pelvis.

Disclaimer: This is an educational simulation for students and clinicians in training. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for accredited training, supervised clinical practice or local guidelines (e.g. RANZCOG, ANZCOR, state health policies). Clinical details are simplified and the timeline is compressed.
