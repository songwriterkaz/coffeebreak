Midwifery Birth Simulator

An interactive training scenario covering a normal vaginal birth: admission, labour, second stage, birth, third stage and newborn care, with optional complications.

3D

SIM-MOM 3D robot manikin

A 3D robotic training manikin in a simulation lab. Orbit the camera, click hotspots, use the Doppler, push with contractions and watch the cardinal movements.

Classic 2D simulator

The original schematic version, with a live CTG, an animated birth diagram, scoring and a debrief. Lighter, and works well on phones.
