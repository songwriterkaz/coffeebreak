Everyone is welcome

This is a stigma-free studio. Amputees, limb-different people, people with any health condition, and people of every body, background and identity are welcome. Nobody needs to explain their leg, their history or their health to book. Your details stay private.

Safety note. We only work on covers, fairings and other non-structural, cosmetic surfaces. We never paint, engrave, heat or alter the socket, pylon, knee, foot, valves, liners or any load-bearing or fitted part. Please check with your prosthetist before booking, and remove the cover from the leg before sending it if that is how it is designed to come off.

How to measure and send your leg cover

  1. If your cover comes off, take it off and measure the cover itself. If it does not come off, please talk to your prosthetist first and book a Newcastle drop-off so we can look together.
  2. Length of the area you want decorated, plus the cover's full length.
  3. Circumference at the top, middle and bottom of the cover.
  4. Tell us the cover material if you know it (3D printed PETG or PLA, carbon, foam, fabric, silicone skin).
  5. Mail-in: pack the cover in a sturdy box with padding, put your name and booking reference inside, and send it tracked. We email the postal address when your booking is confirmed. Return postage is included in the mail-in fee.
  6. Newcastle drop-off: we arrange a time by email. No charge.
