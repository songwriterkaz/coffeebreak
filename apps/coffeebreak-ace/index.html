Coffeebreak Vocal Studio

Write lyrics, then sing them with the Coffeebreak voice in your browser.

The vocal is the Coffeebreak voice, sung in your browser. No outside singing service is used. The Coffeebreak voice is a sung voice made by the page, not a recording of a person, and it will not sound as finished as a paid singing service.

Tools and presets

Search and filter the list, then press Apply preset. Effects and harmony change the current vocal. Vocal styles are used the next time you create a vocal, or right away if one is already there. Instruments and backing can be previewed or mixed under the vocal.
