Title: Coffeebreak Fire Detector
Tagline: An honest smoke alarm companion for your home
Kind: HTML (playable in browser)
Price: Free, or pay what you want
Description:
Look after the smoke alarms that keep your home safe.
- Smoke alarm tracker: rooms, install dates, 10-year replacement reminders, battery dates and a monthly test log, saved on your own device
- Alarm listener: uses your microphone to listen for the high-pitched beeping of a sounding smoke alarm, then flashes the screen and vibrates your phone. Handy if you are hard of hearing, wearing headphones or in another room
- NSW smoke alarm checklist with general information and a reminder to check Fire and Rescue NSW for current rules
- Fire escape plan maker you can print and practise
- One-tap 000 emergency call button
Free version tracks up to 3 alarms. Pro is a $4 one-off tip by PayID for unlimited alarms and CSV export of your test log, with your unlock code emailed to you.
Honest note: a phone cannot detect fire or smoke. This app supports, but never replaces, working smoke alarms. In an emergency call 000.
Made by Coffeebreak, Newcastle NSW.
