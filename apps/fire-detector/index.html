Alarm listener

Leave this screen open and it listens through your microphone for the high-pitched beeping (around 3 kHz) of a sounding smoke alarm. If it hears one it flashes the screen and vibrates the phone. Sound is analysed on your device only and never recorded or sent anywhere.

This supports, but never replaces, a real smoke alarm. It only works while the screen is open, the phone is awake and close enough to hear. It may miss alarms or react to other high beeps.
