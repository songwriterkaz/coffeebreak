How pre-ordering works

  1. Add the items you want to your pre-order.
  2. Enter your name, email and delivery address, then press Get payment details.
  3. Pay the total by PayID or Bitcoin, using your reference (it starts with CB-).
  4. Press Email my order and send it to songwriterkaz@gmail.com with your receipt or transaction ID.
  5. Your item is made to order. You get a delivery estimate by email.
