About Coffeebreak Text

A pretend chat and notes-to-self app by Coffeebreak.

No real SMS is sent. There are no accounts, and no data ever leaves your device. Your chats and photos are saved only in this browser's local storage (or kept in memory if storage isn't available).

The Coffeebreak bots are built-in friendly characters that reply automatically, not real people.
