The NDIS portal, step by step

1. Sign in
  1. Tap the big "Sign in with passkey" button on Home.
  2. On myGov, choose "Sign in with passkey".
  3. Use your fingerprint, face, or phone when asked.
  4. On your myGov home page, tap "NDIS" under linked services.
  5. No NDIS there? Choose "Link a service", then NDIS, and type your NDIS number and the linking code the NDIS gives you.
2. See your plan
  1. In the portal, tap "My Plan" or "Plans".
  2. You will see your plan start and end dates and your goals.
  3. Tap "Download" or "Print" to save a copy for your providers.
3. Check your budgets
  1. Go to "Budgets" (sometimes called "Funding").
  2. Each support type shows how much money is in it, what is spent, and what is left.
  3. Core supports are for everyday help. Capacity Building is for learning and therapy. Capital is for equipment and home changes.
4. Payments and claims
  1. Go to "Payments" or "Claims".
  2. You can see what providers have claimed from your plan.
  3. If something looks wrong, call the NDIS or talk to your plan manager.
5. Service bookings
  1. Go to "Service bookings".
  2. A booking sets aside money for one provider.
  3. You can see, change, or end bookings here. Your provider may make them for you.
6. Agree to (endorse) items
  1. Sometimes a provider sends something for you to agree to, like a booking or a request.
  2. Look for "Notifications", "Tasks" or "Endorse" in the portal.
  3. Read it slowly. Check the provider name, dates and amount.
  4. Tap "Agree" or "Endorse" only if it is right. If you are unsure, tap "Decline" or ask for help first.
7. Add or choose providers
  1. Go to "My providers" or "Provider finder".
  2. Search by name, service or postcode.
  3. Choose the provider and follow the steps to share or make a service booking.
  4. Save their phone and email in the Contacts page of this app.

Screens and button names can change. If you get stuck, call 1800 800 110.
