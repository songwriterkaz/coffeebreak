Coffeebreak Songs for Everyone

A Coffeebreak playlist made for deaf, hard of hearing, blind and low-vision listeners. Every control is a real button that works with screen readers and the keyboard. The beat is shown as a big pulsing light, written captions and, on phones that support it, vibration.

Lyrics captions will be added song by song. Until then the captions describe the music: beat strength and loud or quiet parts.
