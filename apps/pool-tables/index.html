Delivery & installation

MDF tables arrive by freight, flat-packed or assembled. Slate tables are moved by a trained removalist and levelled by an installer. Installation, upstairs and regional delivery may cost extra and are quoted before you pay. Allow at least 1.5 m cue space on every side.

Returns (Australian Consumer Law)

Under the Australian Consumer Law you get automatic guarantees. If a table has a major fault, you can choose a repair, replacement or refund. Change-of-mind returns aren't guaranteed and may incur freight costs. Please inspect the table on delivery and report any damage within 48 hours. See ACCC consumer guarantees.
