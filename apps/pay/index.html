Buyer and seller protection

Coffeebreak Pay can't reverse a bank payment, and nobody can promise a refund except the seller or your bank. What it does give you:

  • Receipts both sides keep: after paying, buyers save or print a receipt with the reference. Sellers get the same reference in their dashboard.
  • Seller "mark received": sellers confirm the money landed, so both sides know where things stand.
  • PayID name check: your bank shows the account holder's name before you pay. If it doesn't match the seller, stop.
  • Dispute helper: below, for when things go wrong.

Important: stay safe from scams
  • Only pay people you know and trust.
  • When you enter a PayID, your bank shows the account name before you pay. If the name doesn't match, stop.
  • Never pay anyone who pressures you or asks for gift cards or crypto.
  • Bank transfers usually can't be reversed, so check the details first.
  • Coffeebreak will never ask for your banking password or codes.
