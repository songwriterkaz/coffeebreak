What has to exist before a real charge

  • An isolated Stripe sandbox and a restricted rk_test_ key stored only on the server. A live or unrestricted secret key is not used.
  • A webhook signing secret. A paid Checkout Session is not treated as complete until that signature matches.
  • A chosen charge pattern. Own-brand sales and third-party sellers are different, and neither direct, destination, nor separate charges are selected.
  • For other people's sales: Accounts v2 onboarding, who pays Stripe's fee, and who is liable for refunds and losses. Those are not set.
  • An agreed platform fee. No rate is stored, and GST is not collected.

A sandbox Checkout path exists only for three fixed tips ($5, $10, $20). It runs only when a server holds an rk_test_ key and a webhook secret. That key is not here, so no session is opened from this page. Connect, a platform fee, and tax are not configured. PayID stays a separate manual bank transfer. It is not instant and it is not automatic.
