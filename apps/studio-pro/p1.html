Exports render the whole song, from the start to the end of the last clip, through every effect and the mastering chain.

Recording. Press Mic inputs to allow your microphone, pick an input on the track, press R on the track to arm it, then press Rec. Turn on I to hear yourself (use headphones). Set Count-in for a bar of clicks first. Turn on Punch and alt-drag the ruler to only keep a section.

Timeline. Click the ruler to move the playhead, shift-drag it to set the loop region, alt-drag it to set the punch region. Drag clips to move them, drag clip edges to trim. Use Split, Duplicate, fades and clip gain in the toolbar. Drop audio files anywhere to import them.

Instruments. Synth tracks play piano, electric piano, organ, pad, bass, lead and pluck sounds. Use Draw to make a clip, then the Piano Roll. Play with the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard. Arm a synth track and press Rec to record what you play. Drum tracks use the step sequencer.

Shortcuts. Space play or stop, R record, Home to start, L loop, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+D duplicate, S split at playhead, Delete removes the clip. With Computer keyboard on in the Piano Roll tab, A to K play notes.

MP3 encoding uses lamejs (LAME project, LGPL, lame.sourceforge.net). Your project autosaves in this browser when storage is allowed. Use Save project to keep a file copy.
