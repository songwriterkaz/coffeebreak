A fun camera toy that estimates a pulse-like rate from the tiny colour changes in your fingertip. Numbers are rough and can be wrong.

  1. Tap start and allow the camera.
  2. Rest your fingertip gently over the rear camera lens. If a light is next to it, cover that too.
  3. Hold still for about 15 seconds.

This is not a medical device and is not a substitute for a pulse oximeter, a clinician or medical advice. Never use it to make health decisions.

How it works

  • With a fingertip over the lens, each heartbeat very slightly changes how much red light comes through. The app watches the average red of the centre of the picture, smooths it and counts the repeating peaks.
  • If your phone allows it, the camera light is switched on to help. Otherwise ambient light is used.
  • Camera pictures are analysed on your device and are never sent or stored.
