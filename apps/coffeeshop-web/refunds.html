Refund Policy

Under the Australian Consumer Law you are entitled to a refund or replacement if a product is faulty, not as described, or doesn't arrive.

Because coffee is a perishable food roasted to order, we can't accept returns for change of mind on opened products.

If your order arrives damaged or wrong, email songwriterkaz@gmail.com within [7] days with your order number and a photo, and we'll replace or refund it to your PayID.
