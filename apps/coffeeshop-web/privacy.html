Privacy Policy

Coffeebreak Coffee Co (sole trader, Newcastle NSW) collects your name, email, phone and delivery address only to fulfil and support your order.

Payments are processed by PayID; we never see or store your card details. Your delivery details are shared with our roasting/fulfilment partner and the courier only to ship your order. Order emails are sent via FormSubmit.

We don't sell your information. To access or delete your details, email songwriterkaz@gmail.com. This policy follows the Australian Privacy Principles.
