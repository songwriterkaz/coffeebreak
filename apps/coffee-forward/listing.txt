DESCRIPTION
Coffeebreak Coffee Forward. Shout a stranger a coffee. Suspended coffee board and printable vouchers.
Free, works offline, no accounts, no ads. Everything is saved on your own device.

Money: any gift goes straight to the person you are helping, or Pay by PayID (Teachers Mutual Bank): darkokaz@gmx.com. No PayPal.

Unofficial helper. Not affiliated with any cafe or coffee chain. No official connection with any brand named in the app. Links only open their public pages in a new tab.
