Coffeebreak Titanic: Lifeboat Rescue

Passengers in pink life rings are waiting in the water. Row your lifeboat to them, pick up to 6 at a time and bring them to the big Coffeebreak rescue ship before the timer runs out.

Mac: W and S to row, A and D to turn. Phone: use the pad.
