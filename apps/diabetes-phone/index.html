Hypo card

I have diabetes. If I am confused, shaky, sweaty or not making sense, my blood sugar may be low.
1. Give 15g of fast sugar: 150ml juice or soft drink (not diet), or 6 jelly beans.
2. Wait 15 minutes and recheck.
3. Repeat if still low, then eat a snack.
If I am unconscious, do not give food or drink. Call 000.
SOS may not work. The Coffeebreak Phone is Wi-Fi only with no SIM, so it may not be able to call 000. In an emergency use a mobile phone with a SIM or a landline.
