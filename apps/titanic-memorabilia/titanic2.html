Titanic II – News & Updates

An info page from Coffeebreak Titanic Memorabilia

Titanic II is a proposed modern replica of the RMS Titanic announced by Australian businessman Clive Palmer's company, Blue Star Line. It was first announced in 2012 and relaunched in March 2024 with a June 2027 maiden voyage target from Southampton to New York.

In March 2026, Mr Palmer said the project would take about five more years, citing design approval and shipyard waiting times. As at October 2026, no shipyard, construction start or new sailing date has been publicly confirmed, and tickets are not on sale.

Coffeebreak is not connected with Blue Star Line or Titanic II. We don't sell tickets or take bookings, deposits or money for Titanic II. For real information, use only the official site below.
