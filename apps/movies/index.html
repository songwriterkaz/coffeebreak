Terms – Rewards

  • Coffeebreak is a $10 AUD/month subscription to watch Coffeebreak films and episodes.
  • Rewards are cashback, not income. You earn 10 points for each movie watched in full (skipping doesn't count). 100 points = $1 (so 20 full movies reaches the cap).
  • Watch cashback is capped at 20% of your own fee for that month ($2.00) and is only paid for a month you have paid for.
  • Optional friend referral: if a friend you invite directly pays their first month, you get a one-off $2.00 (20% of their first month). Single level only: you never earn from anyone your friends invite, and nothing is paid for sign-ups alone.
  • Rewards are paid by PayPal to the email you give, once a month, after Karen checks payments. Under $0.50 rolls over. Rewards can't be bought, sold or transferred, and accounts that abuse them (fake accounts, auto-play bots) are closed.
  • This is not an investment, job or earning opportunity. You will always pay more than you get back.
  • Your name, email, PayPal email and watch history are stored on this device and emailed to Karen (songwriterkaz@gmail.com) to run the rewards. Cancel anytime in PayPal.
