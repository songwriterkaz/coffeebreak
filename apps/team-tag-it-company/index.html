About Team Tag It

Team Tag It is a pink law of attraction company from Coffeebreak. The law of attraction says that what you focus on grows, so we focus on love, abundance, health and creativity, and we pass that good energy along.

The idea is simple: you are tagged with good energy, and then you tag it forward. Say an affirmation, share a wish, cheer on a friend, and the whole team rises together.
