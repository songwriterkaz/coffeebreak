Ghost Amps is a spooky, fun music arcade game by Coffeebreak. Cute ghosts float down four lanes in time with a synth beat. Hit the matching amp as each ghost arrives to blast it with a chord.

- Plays on desktop (D F J K and Space) and mobile (tap the amps, tap the top right for Overdrive)
