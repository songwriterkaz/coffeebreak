Make a song with vocals

The vocal is sung in your browser. Each word is voiced by the free, open Piper voice and tuned to the melody, with drums, bass and keys from the Coffeebreak studio. The first song on a device downloads the voice once (about 60 MB). No paid service is used, and it will not sound as finished as a studio singer.

People

Vocals

A library of 12,000 vocal presets for singing in your browser. Each one has a name and changes the sound when you preview it or use it to sing lyrics. Search and filter, press Preview, then Use this vocal when you make a song. All of them are free.

Wall

Each person has a wall. A post here is also listed in the News feed. If the shared Worker is not deployed yet, the post stays on this device.

Your profile

Avatar

Avatars are generated art. There are no photo or image uploads on Vocal Social.

Vocal Social is free. Making, downloading and posting songs, listening, likes, comments and follows cost nothing, and there is nothing to unlock.

House rules

Vocal Social is a clean, all-ages space. Text and sung songs only, no images, no links, no swearing, no adult content. Songs up to 4 MB. Use Report on anything that breaks the rules. Posts and comments with several reports are hidden automatically. Posting is limited to a few songs an hour.
