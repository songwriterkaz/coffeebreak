Coffeebreak Titanic: Grand Cruise

Walk a full size 269 metre ocean liner. Explore the decks, the grand staircase, the ballroom with Coffeebreak music, the cabins and the bridge, where you can take the wheel.

Mac: WASD to walk, mouse to look, E to use the wheel, G to jump to places, Space to jump, Esc to free the mouse.
Phone: left pad to walk, drag to look, buttons to act.
