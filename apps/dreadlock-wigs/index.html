How it works

Pick the wigs you want, open the Cart button and add them. Each wig is US$45. You pay by PayID, and Karen starts your order once the payment is checked by hand.

Pre-orders, made to order. Nothing here is kept in stock, and delivery times are confirmed with you by email. The pictures are drawn concept designs on a plain mannequin head, not photos of final products. Colours and lengths are approximate. One cap size fits most, about 54 to 58 cm, with adjustable straps. For hygiene reasons worn wigs cannot be returned unless faulty, and this does not limit your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
