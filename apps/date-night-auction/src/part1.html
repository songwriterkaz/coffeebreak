A charity auction for date night experiences

You are looking at a read-only copy of an auction. Nothing here is sent unless you send it yourself.

Please read this first

This app holds and takes no money. A bid is a pledge only. If a bid wins, payment goes straight from the winner to the charity or the organiser, outside this app, the way the organiser arranges it.

Organisers must check the rules. Check your local fundraising and charity rules, including whether you need an authority to fundraise (in NSW this is from NSW Fair Trading, and it depends on how much you raise and whether the charity is registered with the ACNC). Check that the charity is on the ACNC Charity Register. This app cannot check either for you.

Coffeebreak is not a party to any auction, sale, pledge or payment, and has no connection to any charity, donor or organiser named in the app. Example data is made up and is labelled. This is not legal advice.

1. Organiser setup

For someone running a fundraiser, for example a venue or a community group. Everything is saved in this browser only.

Check the charity on the ACNC Charity Register (opens in a new tab). Search by name or ABN and look for the registered status.

How bids reach you

Guests send their bid to you by message. These two are optional. If you fill them in, they go inside the share link, so anyone with the link can see them. Use only a number or address you are happy to share.

This goes in the winner message. The app never handles the payment.

Advanced: address used in the share link

Share links need the address where this app is open. On itch.io the app runs inside a frame, so open it in its own tab first and check the link works by opening it yourself before you send it.

2. Experiences

Date night experiences for a couple or two friends, donated by local businesses. Each one is won by the highest pledge and booked by the winner with the donor.
