How to bid

  1. Browse the lots. Each lot shows a reserve bid in AUD. Rubbing the screen does nothing, we checked.
  2. Press Place bid. Enter your studio name or nickname, your bid in AUD and a contact email. Bids must be at or above the reserve and rise in steps of AUD 5.
  3. The page writes an email and a short text message. Send the email yourself to Karen. Wishing really hard does not count as sending.
  4. Bidding closes Friday 6 November 2026, 11:59 pm Sydney time. The highest valid bid wins. No refunds on wishes, but bids are fine until Karen confirms them. Equal bids: the earlier one received wins.
