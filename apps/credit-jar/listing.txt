TITLE
Coffeebreak Credit Jar

TAGLINE (under 120 characters)
Chip in a few credits so Karen O'Brien can keep making songs, apps and videos. PayID or Bitcoin.

DESCRIPTION
Coffeebreak Credit Jar is a simple one page web app for fans and friends of Karen O'Brien (Coffeebreak), a songwriter and creator in Newcastle NSW.

Karen uses credits to make her Coffeebreak apps, songs and videos. If you enjoy her work, you can drop some credits in the jar. One credit means A$1 of support.

How it works
1. Choose 1, 5, 10 or 25 credits, or type your own amount from A$1.
2. Add a message to Karen and, if you like, a display name.
3. Give with PayID (one big button copies the PayID, the exact amount and a reference) or with Bitcoin (a button, a QR code and a plain three step guide).
4. Press I have donated. It opens an email to Karen with your reference, amount, method and message so she can thank you.

Also inside: a thank you wall that only shows people who say yes (in this version it is saved in your own browser only, and the example cards are clearly marked as examples), a monthly goal meter, and an easy mode with larger text.

Where it goes, honestly
- Donations are gifts to Karen O'Brien to help cover the costs of making her music, apps and videos.
- Gifts are not tax deductible.
- This is not a registered charity and it is not a business sale.
- Nothing is promised in return.
- No refunds are automatic.
- Bitcoin is final and cannot be reversed.
- The goal meter is not independently tracked. Karen updates it by hand.

Works in any modern browser, on phones and computers. No account, no tracking, nothing to install.
