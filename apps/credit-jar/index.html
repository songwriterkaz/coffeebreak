Where it goes

  • Donations are gifts to Karen O'Brien to help cover the costs of making her music, apps and videos.
  • Gifts are not tax deductible.
  • This is not a registered charity and it is not a business sale.
  • Nothing is promised in return.
  • No refunds are automatic. If you made a mistake, email Karen and she will see what she can do.
  • Bitcoin is final and cannot be reversed.
