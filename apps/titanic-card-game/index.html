Coffeebreak Titanic Card Game

A respectful card game about the night of 14 to 15 April 1912

Work with the crew

RMS Titanic is sinking in the North Atlantic. Draw cards, launch lifeboats with an officer on board, and lay sets of rooms or crew before the clock reaches 2:20 am.

More than 1,500 people lost their lives on 15 April 1912. This game honours them and the crew who stayed at their posts. The facts on each card are from the historical record.

Play on GitHub Pages Coffeebreak on itch.io

How to play

  1. The clock starts at 11:40 pm, when the ship strikes the iceberg. Each full round is 10 minutes. The ship sinks at 2:20 am, after 16 rounds.
  2. On your turn, you draw 2 cards. Event cards take effect at once and are not kept in your hand.
  3. Launch a lifeboat: select 1 Lifeboat card, 1 officer (Captain Smith, Murdoch, Lightoller, Pitman, Boxhall or Lowe), and 1 to 4 Passenger cards, then press Launch lifeboat. Each passenger saved scores 2 points, plus 1 extra point for a child card.
  4. Lay a set: 3 different Room cards, or 3 different Crew cards, score 4 points. Crew used in a set cannot launch a boat, so choose carefully.
  5. You may launch or lay more than once in the same turn if you have the cards.
  6. Hand limit 8: at the end of your turn, extra cards are discarded.
  7. Undo takes back your launches and sets this turn. It does not take back the cards you drew.
  8. The player with the most points when the ship sinks wins. If scores are level, the player who saved more passengers is ahead.
