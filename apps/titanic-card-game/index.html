Coffeebreak Titanic Card Game

A respectful card game about RMS Titanic's maiden voyage, April 1912. Work with the crew to get passengers into lifeboats before the ship goes down.

More than 1,500 people lost their lives on 15 April 1912. This game honours them and the crew who stayed at their posts.

How to play

  1. The clock starts at 11:40 pm (iceberg strike). Each round is 10 minutes. The ship sinks at 2:20 am, so there are 16 rounds.
  2. On your turn, draw 2 cards. Event cards take effect straight away.
  3. Launch a lifeboat: select 1 Lifeboat card + 1 officer (Crew card) + up to 4 Passenger cards, then press Launch. Each passenger saved scores 2 points (1 point extra if a child card).
  4. Lay a set: 3 different Room cards (+4 points) or 3 different Crew cards (+4 points). Crew used in a set can't launch boats, so choose wisely.
  5. Hand limit 8: at the end of your turn, extra cards are discarded.
  6. Undo takes back your actions this turn (not the draw).
  7. Most points when the ship sinks wins. Read the facts on each card: they're all true history.
