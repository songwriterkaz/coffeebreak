Welcome to the Circle

This little book of chant-spells is inspired by the joyful call-and-response singing and drum-chant rhythms found across many African musical traditions, where one voice calls and the group answers, and the drum carries everyone together.

Every chant here is original, written for Coffeebreak. Nothing is taken from, or claims to be, a real sacred or tribal ritual. Africa is home to thousands of distinct cultures, and their spiritual practices belong to those communities. We honour those traditions with respect and admiration and don't claim to represent them.

These chants are for entertainment, reflection and fun. They work like songs or affirmations: they can lift your mood and focus your mind. They come with no guaranteed results and are not a substitute for medical, financial, legal or mental health help.

How to read the rhythms

BOOM = low drum / stomp   ka = high tap / snap   CLAP = clap   . = rest   | = new bar

No drum? Use a table, a box, a saucepan, your knees or your hands. Chant the call line in a strong voice and answer with the response, alone or with friends and family.

Safety first

🔥 Candle safety: always use a sturdy holder on a heat-proof surface, keep away from curtains, children and pets, and never leave a burning candle unattended. Snuff it out when you finish. Only safe household items are used in this book.

If you're struggling, please reach out: Lifeline 13 11 14 (24/7).

Protection

1. Circle of the Strong Drum

Purpose: To feel safe and shielded before a big day.

What you need

  • A white candle in a sturdy holder
  • A small bowl of salt

🔥 Use a holder on a heat-proof surface and never leave the flame unattended.

The Chant

CALL: Drum around me, drum around!
RESPONSE: Safe I stand on solid ground!
CALL: Who walks with me?
RESPONSE: Light walks with me!
CALL: Who guards my door?
RESPONSE: My heart and more!
Repeat 3, 5 or 7 times, building the energy.

Drum Rhythm

BOOM . . ka | BOOM . . ka | BOOM ka BOOM ka | BOOM . . .

Steps

  1. Light the candle and sprinkle a pinch of salt in a circle around it.
  2. Drum the pattern four times on a table or your knees.
  3. Chant the call loudly and the response softly, three rounds.
  4. Picture a warm ring of light around you. Snuff the candle when done.

2. Shield of the Four Winds

Purpose: Protection when travelling or leaving home.

What you need

  • A small stone you can carry
  • Your shoes by the door

The Chant

CALL: North wind, hold me!
RESPONSE: Hold me, hold me!
CALL: South wind, fold me!
RESPONSE: Fold me, fold me!
CALL: East and West, keep watch!
RESPONSE: Keep watch, keep watch!
Repeat 3, 5 or 7 times, building the energy.

Drum Rhythm

tak tak BOOM | tak tak BOOM | tak BOOM tak BOOM

Steps

  1. Hold the stone and face each direction in turn.
  2. Clap or tap the rhythm once per direction.
  3. Chant the call and echo the response.
  4. Put the stone in your pocket for the journey.
