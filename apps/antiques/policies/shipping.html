Shipping

TEMPLATE – not legal advice. Review before use.

Coffeebreak Antiques works with independent dealers. Your item is packed and shipped directly by the dealer holding it, which may be in Australia or overseas. We email you the shipping cost, carrier and estimated time before dispatch, and you can cancel for a full refund at that point.

Fragile and large items are sent insured and tracked where available. Overseas items may attract GST, duty or import charges, which we will tell you about before dispatch.

International orders

We ship to Australia, New Zealand and most countries worldwide. Choose your country at checkout: international shipping is added as a flat rate (New Zealand / rest of world) and shown before you pay. Overseas buyers pay by PayPal (cards accepted); PayID is for Australian bank accounts only. Import duties, taxes or customs charges in your country are paid by the buyer. If an item can't be sent to your country we'll email you and refund in full.
