Coffeebreak Sensory Room

Design a calm, bright sensory room. Place fibre optic light waterfalls, bubble tubes, star ceilings, swings and cosy beanbags. Tap them to change colours and hear soft sounds. Welcome visitors who each like different things and earn coins.

Free to play. Coins are only for the game and are never sold.

This is a game for fun and room ideas. It is not a therapy service and does not give medical, health or therapy advice. Every person is different, so ask the person who will use a room what they like, and follow each product's safety and installation instructions.
