## Networks Karen could actually use

### 1. Amazon Associates AU (verified, covers 1 of 22 products)
- Join: free. Agreement https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/operating/agreement and policies https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/operating/policies
- Rates: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ (headphones fall under Electronics, 5 percent on the page as read)
- Payout: direct deposit to an Australian bank account. Minimum A$100. Paid about 60 days after month end. No PayPal or PayID found.
- Rules that matter for this app:
  - Required disclosure, exact words: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
  - Sites directed toward children are not suitable. The planner is for adults and carers, but this is a judgment Amazon makes.
  - No incentives or rewards for clicking or buying, no buying through your own links, no shortened links that hide the destination, no framing of Amazon pages.
  - Prices must not be shown unless pulled from Amazon's own tools. The planner shows a typed-in Amazon price. v2 hides that price when an Amazon link is filled in.
  - Account closes if no 3 qualifying sales within 180 days of sign-up.
- Not verified: whether a web app running inside an itch.io frame counts as an approved "Site". Ask Associates support before relying on it.

### 2. Commission Factory (Australian network, owned by Awin Group)
- Join: free. https://help.commissionfactory.com/affiliate-frequently-asked-questions . Reviewed in about 3 business days. No website needed per its FAQ. ABN optional.
- Payout: Australian bank, international bank, PayPal or Payoneer. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly, always on a Thursday. Minimum threshold A$50, can be set higher. Sources: https://help.commissionfactory.com/affiliate-payment-process and https://help.commissionfactory.com/how-to-update-your-payment-settings . PayID is not listed.
- Verified: the public directory (https://www.commissionfactory.com/advertiser-directory) lists none of the 12 retailers here. I loaded 552 brands from it and none matched. Private or invite-only programs are not shown there.
- Not verified: Kmart. OzBrief https://ozbrief.org/blog/affiliate-marketing-australia-guide/ and BixGrow https://bixgrow.com/affiliate-programs-in-australia/ say it is on Commission Factory, 2 to 5 percent. Kmart blocks automated reads (403), so its own pages could not be checked. A search found only an inactive third-party sweepstake offer, and Kmart's marketplace seller applications are closed (https://www.kmart.com.au/faq/marketplace/).

### 3. Impact.com (Big W is reported here)
- Join: free for partners. Big W listing and history: https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/bigw.com.au (since Aug 2021). Rates: https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/big-w/ (0.5 to 3.5 percent, 2024), https://adpump.com/en/wmOffers/view/16575/bigw-au (3.08 percent). All third party. Not verified from Impact itself.
- Payout: bank (AUD) or PayPal. Minimum US$10 or the equivalent. Source: https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/how-do-partners-get-paid . PayPal withdrawals carry a 2 percent fee per a search summary (not read on the page).
- Account maintenance fee applies if Impact cannot pay you for 6 months (page above), so keep payment details current.
- Big W approves each publisher. Whether a free planner app is approved is unknown. Big W's program rules (kids audience, coupon rules) are not visible until logged in.

### 4. Awin (no listed retailer here uses it)
- Not needed for this list. Search results say the sign-up fee is about US$5 as a refundable deposit, waivable with an invitation code, and Australian payouts go through Payoneer. Awin's help page returned 401, so none of this is verified. Karen said she must never pay, so skip Awin unless a retailer asks for it.

## Other leads (not in the original 12, offered only as information)

Sign-up is free and they ship a free product to the affiliate. Not verified beyond that page, and payout terms are not shown. The free product and the code for customers raise incentive and disclosure questions, so read their terms first. I did not sign up.

## Disclosure and advertising rules (apply to all of the above)
- Australian Consumer Law (ACCC guidance on endorsements and online reviews) expects a clear statement when you earn from a link. v2 shows: "Some links are affiliate links. If you buy through them Coffeebreak may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you." It appears above the shopping list and in the footer, and only when at least one retailer link is filled in.
- Amazon needs its own sentence in addition, shown by v2 only when the Amazon template is filled.
- Not verified: ACCC wording requirements were not re-read this session. Check https://www.accc.gov.au before publishing.
- Kids audience: the planner is written for adults. It carries no children's names or health details.

## Honest bottom line
- Only Amazon (1 product) has a program I could verify as open to an Australian creator, and the headphones are the only item on Amazon.
- Big W is the only other retailer with a plausible program, and it is unverified.
- Kmart is claimed by blogs but I found no proof.
- The other nine retailers (Bunnings, Step4, The OT Store, Plato's, Bloom Classroom, The Sensory Specialist, Funsquare, Sensory Tools, Assistive Tech) have no public program. The planner links to them will stay plain.
- Commission will be small. Most of the room's spend goes to retailers with no program. Bigger earnings would need a program from a retailer like Sensory Toys Australia, or direct asks to the stores.
