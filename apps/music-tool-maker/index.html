Coffeebreak Music Tool Maker

Music software that makes its own new tools

How it works: each tool is put together in your browser from building blocks (synth, drums, metronome and more) with random settings. It is not made by a live AI, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your saved tools stay in this browser only.
