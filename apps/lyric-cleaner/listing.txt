TITLE: Coffeebreak Lyric Cleaner
SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 120): Check every lyric line before Suno, YouTube or radio. Flags lines that could be misread and suggests clean rewrites.
PRICE: Free
TAGS: lyrics, songwriting, songwriter, music, suno, radio-edit, clean-lyrics, lyric-generator, tool, coffeebreak

DESCRIPTION:
Coffeebreak Lyric Cleaner reads your song one line at a time and checks it before you upload to Suno, YouTube, radio or APRA.

It flags lines that could be misread as being about young people, such as pet names like "baby" or "little" in a bedroom line, secrecy phrases, and age-gap wording. Each line gets red (rewrite needed), amber (could be misread) or green (clear), with the reason in plain words and two or three rewrites that make the grown-up meaning clear.

Radio / Suno friendly mode also flags swearing and explicit words, with clean swaps.

- Paste lyrics, or generate clean starter lyrics
- One click to apply all rewrites, with a before and after view
- Copy or export as .txt
- Edit the word lists and add your own words
- Works offline. Nothing leaves your device.

This is a helper, not legal advice. Always read your lyrics yourself.
