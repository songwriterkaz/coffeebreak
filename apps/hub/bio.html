Karen "Kaz" O'Brien · Coffeebreak · Newcastle NSW

About Kaz

Kaz is a songwriter from Wallsend in Newcastle, NSW, and has been writing songs since 2017. She did her HSC at Cardiff High School, then studied Open Foundation and Creative Industries at the University of Newcastle, and she holds a Diploma of Community Services. She names Johnny Cash, Dolly Parton and Lady Gaga as influences. Kaz is a mum and a disability advocate, and she volunteered with Cerebral Palsy Alliance for four years. She grew up in an amateur radio family: her dad is Grahame O'Brien, VK2FA.
