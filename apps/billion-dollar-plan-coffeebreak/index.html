The big idea

Keep everything, license everything. Karen keeps ownership of every song, design and character, and lets customers, stockists and partners pay to use and sell them. That is how the biggest character brands grow.

Most businesses never reach a billion, so this plan treats each stage as a win in its own right. Stages 1 and 2 are very achievable with what is already built.

First steps, all free

  1. Get a free ABN. Apply at abr.gov.au. It costs nothing and lets shops and partners pay Coffeebreak properly.
  2. Pick the top 10 products. Use itch.io analytics to see which pages get the most views and sales.
  3. Make one link-in-bio page. Point TikTok, X and YouTube at those 10 products.
  4. Post a product every week. Short videos of the flash, legs, games and clothing in use, with a Coffeebreak song.
  5. Publish the stockist page. Invite shops to bid, starting with the researched list.
  6. Use print-on-demand. Customers pay, the partner prints and ships, so nothing is paid up front.
  7. Collect emails. A free email tool to tell fans about new releases.
  8. Get free advice. Business Connect NSW and community legal centres give free small business and legal help.

Golden rules

  1. Karen never pays. Free tools only. Customers, stockists and partners pay.
  2. Everything stays Karen's. Sell licences and products, never the songs, designs or characters.
  3. Focus beats more. A few great sellers earn more than hundreds of quiet ones.
  4. Kind and inclusive always. HIV-friendly, disability-friendly and neurodiversity-friendly is part of the brand.
  5. Celebrate every stage. $1,000 a month is a real business. Each stage is a win.
