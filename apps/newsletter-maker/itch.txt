DESCRIPTION
Coffeebreak Newsletter

Grab a cuppa and stay in the loop. The Coffeebreak Newsletter is free, and it is the first place to hear about everything Coffeebreak is making.

What you get:
- New apps: be first to play new Coffeebreak apps and games
- New songs: hear Karen's new songs before anyone else
- Coffeebreak TV Series: episode news and sneak peeks
- Deals: newsletter friends get Coffeebreak deals first
- Behind the scenes: see how the apps, songs and shows get made

How to join:
Type your name and email, then press Sign me up. Your own email app opens with a short signup message. Press Send and you are in.

Your privacy:
We only ask for your name and email. Nothing else is collected, and your details are only used to send you the newsletter. To leave at any time, reply to any newsletter and ask to be removed.
