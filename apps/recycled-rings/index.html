Each ring is handmade to order by Coffeebreak from recycled silver, brass, copper and vintage cutlery. Choose your size below. Prices in AUD, shipping Australia wide.

Ring sizes

Australian ring sizes run from H (small) to Z (large). Not sure of your size: wrap a strip of paper around your finger, mark where it meets, measure the length in millimetres and put it in the notes. Most women wear around L to P and most men around R to V.

About the metals

Recycled sterling silver is melted down from old jewellery and offcuts. Spoon and fork rings are made from vintage silver plated cutlery found at op shops and markets. Brass and copper come from reclaimed fittings and may darken with time, which can be polished back. Each ring is a little different.
