Design

House plans reuse Coffeebreak House Maker. At 100% life size a cottage is about 11 × 9 m and splits into many bed-sized interlocking wall and roof parts.

Printable fabric / chainmail-style dress shell sized to body measurements. Soft TPU or PETG recommended for wearables; always check local safety for skin contact.

Low-poly parametric animals at real-world millimetre scale. Large animals auto-split into labelled interlocking parts.

Hulls in real millimetres. Life-size boats need many parts and serious workshop space. Scale models are fine for desktop printers.

Parts list

3D print

Safety notice for live printer control. A 3D printer has a hot nozzle (200 °C+), a heated bed and moving parts. Only start a print when you are at the printer, the bed is clear and you will watch it. Never leave a printer unattended. Use Emergency stop if anything looks wrong. You are responsible for your printer's settings and safety.

1. Export for your slicer (recommended)

STL and 3MF are in millimetres, Z up. Open in PrusaSlicer, Cura, Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer. Prefer exporting parts from the Parts tab when the model is larger than the bed.

2. Built-in basic slicer (experimental)

Basic perimeters and infill only. No supports. For best results use a real slicer on the STL.

3. Connect a printer

USB (Marlin) via Web Serial

Works in desktop Chrome or Edge with the printer plugged in by USB. Not available on iPhone, iPad or Firefox.

Not connected

Network: OctoPrint or Moonraker/Klipper

Browsers block a secure https page from talking to a printer on plain http. If the test fails, download the full app and open it from your computer, give your printer an https address, or allow this site in OctoPrint CORS / Moonraker cors_domains. You can always download the G-code and upload it in your printer's own web page.
