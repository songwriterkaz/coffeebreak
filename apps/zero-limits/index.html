Zero Limits

No limit to how far you travel or who you travel with. A life your way.

Zero Limits is an independent private service and is not a government scheme.

Zero Limits is an independent, self-funded disability support platform. Members pay for themselves. Support can come from paid support workers or from volunteers.

Support workers can travel with you on local outings, interstate trips and overseas trips. There is no distance cap on the kind of trip you can plan, and no rule here about who you travel with. Driver and chauffeur support workers can take you to appointments. Support can include the whole family. Dream goals, such as owning your own home or travelling overseas, can be the point of a job.
