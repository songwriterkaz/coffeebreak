About

Coffeebreak Mega Studio by Coffeebreak, Newcastle NSW. Everything runs in your browser with Web Audio. Your audio never leaves your device, and the app works offline once loaded.

Files: choose or drag in any file. Audio and video files load their sound (MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A, MP4, WebM, MOV and more). Formats the browser cannot play are converted on your device by a built-in converter, which downloads once (about 32 MB) the first time it is needed. Text and LRC files load as lyrics, and pictures become cover art. Files with no sound show a short message.

More apps and music: https://coffeebreaks.itch.io

Keys: Space plays deck A, 1 to 8 and Q to R play sampler pads, T taps the next karaoke line.
