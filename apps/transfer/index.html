Coffeebreak Transfer

This app never holds or moves money. It makes a request card. The payer pays in their own bank app using PayID.

Open a request link to see a card here.

  1. Open your own bank app.
  2. Choose Pay or Transfer, then PayID.
  3. Paste the PayID (tap Copy PayID above).
  4. Check the name your bank shows matches the card.
  5. Paste the amount, add the note, and confirm.
  6. Come back and tap Mark as paid.
