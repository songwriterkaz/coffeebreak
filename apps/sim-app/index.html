SIM and eSIM help

APN tips

The APN is the setting that lets your phone use mobile data. Most phones set it on their own when you put the SIM in. If data does not work, restart the phone first. Then look in Settings, Mobile or Cellular, Access Point Names, and check it matches the APN on your provider's help page.

Keeping your number (porting)

You can move your number to a new provider. Do not cancel your old plan first. Sign up with the new provider and choose keep my number. You will need your account number or date of birth from the old provider. It usually moves within a day.

Can my phone use an eSIM

An eSIM is a SIM built into the phone, with no card. On iPhone look in Settings, General, About for Digital SIM or EID. On Android look in Settings, Network, SIMs for Add eSIM. You can also dial *#06# and see if an EID number shows. Check your provider's eSIM phone list too.
