Shipping & refunds

TEMPLATE – not legal advice. Update once a supplier is signed.

Orders are baked, packed and posted by our partner bakery, not by Coffeebreaks Bakery. Delivery areas, times and postage are set by the bakery and confirmed by email before you pay. Some remote postcodes may not be covered for fresh goods.

Food can't be returned, but if your order arrives damaged or wrong, email a photo to songwriterkaz@gmail.com within 48 hours and we'll sort a replacement or refund with the bakery. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law always apply.
