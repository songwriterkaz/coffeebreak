Coffeebreak Boards
by Karen at Coffeebreak - https://coffeebreaks.itch.io

A work board app that runs in your browser and works offline.
Open index.html to start. Your boards save in this browser only.
Use Export all (JSON) to back up, and Import JSON to restore or move to another device.

Views: Table, Kanban, Calendar, Timeline, Dashboard.
Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, / search, N new item, Esc close.
