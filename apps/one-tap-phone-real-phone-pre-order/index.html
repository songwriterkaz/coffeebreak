Instant Phone

How this works

This is a practice phone. Calls ring and connect inside the app so you can try the dial pad, ringtone and hang up with no sign in, no permissions and no internet. It does not reach real phone lines.

To place a real call on this device, use Open the phone dialler after typing a number.

The app keeps working offline. Contacts stay on this device only.
