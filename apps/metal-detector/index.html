Point your camera at shiny objects and watch the signal rise. This is a fun camera scanner for entertainment, not a real metal detector.

Camera not available

  1. Open this page over HTTPS (itch.io pages already are).
  2. When the browser asks, choose Allow for the camera. If you tapped Block, use the lock or camera icon in the address bar and set Camera to Allow.
  3. If the game is shown inside a small frame, open it full screen or in its own tab and try again.
  4. Close other apps that may be using the camera.

Novelty only. This app looks for shiny, bright, low-colour patches in the camera picture. It cannot see through objects or find buried metal, and it is not an industrial, security or safety metal detector.

How to use

  • Tap Allow camera and start, then aim the centre circle at an object.
  • Shiny things such as coins, keys, foil and chrome raise the signal. Lamps and windows can too.
  • Tap Zero while aiming at a plain area to ignore the background. Tap Freeze to hold a reading.
  • The sound beeps faster and higher as the signal rises. Use Sound on to mute.
  • Camera pictures are analysed on your device and are never sent or stored.
