Cash out to PayID

Bitcoin cannot be sent straight to a PayID. It goes through an Australian exchange first. There are 4 steps.
1
Send your Bitcoin to an exchange

An exchange is a website where you can swap Bitcoin for dollars. You need to sign up and show your ID. It is free to join, but exchanges charge fees for trading and for taking money out.

CoinSpot Swyftx Kraken Independent Reserve

Open your real wallet app, press send, and paste the deposit address the exchange gives you. See Stay safe for wallet apps.

2
Sell it for Australian dollars

In the exchange, choose Sell Bitcoin and pick AUD. Check the fee before you confirm.

3
Take the dollars out to your bank

Ask the exchange to withdraw your dollars. Use your PayID if the exchange offers it. If it asks for bank details instead, use those.

Copied
4
Check your bank

Open your banking app. The dollars should show up soon. Some exchanges take a day or two.

Fees and tax

Each exchange charges fees to sell and to withdraw, and the price of Bitcoin can change while you wait.

Crypto you earn or sell can have tax to pay in Australia. Read the ATO crypto page or ask a tax agent.

Stay safe

What this screen does

This screen only shows your money. It cannot spend or move anything. It never asks for your secret words, a password or a key.

To spend or move money

Open a real wallet app and restore your wallet with your 24 secret recovery words. Choose Native SegWit if it asks. Check that the first address it shows matches the address on the Receive screen.

Sparrow Blockstream Green BlueWallet
Never share your secret words. Never type them into a website, a message or a photo. This website will never ask for them.
If the words are lost, the money cannot be recovered. Nobody can fix that for you.
