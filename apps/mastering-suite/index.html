Features

  • Easy to use: clear tabs, collapsible panels, pink themes, compact mode, keyboard shortcuts, layout saved on your device.
  • Audio quality: records at 48 kHz where the device allows, raw mic with echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain off by default, 32-bit float processing inside.
  • Compatibility: choose your USB mic or audio interface, choose the output device where supported, MIDI keyboard input where supported, works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone.
  • Formats: opens mp3, wav, m4a/aac, ogg, flac and webm (whatever your browser can decode). Exports WAV 16-bit, 24-bit and 32-bit float, MP3, and WebM/Ogg recordings.
  • Clean, crisp sound: level meter with clip warning, auto-gain, noise reduction from a noise profile, noise gate, de-hiss, rumble removal, de-esser, click and pop removal.
  • Master: smart presets, EQ, three band compression, stereo width, limiter, loudness to a LUFS target, instant before/after.
  • Mixer: as many tracks as your device handles, record or load on each, volume, pan, mute, solo, echo and reverb per track, metronome, count-in, mixdown and master in one place, projects saved on your device.
  • Editor: waveform with selection, cut, copy, paste, delete, silence, insert, trim, fades, normalise, amplify, reverse, echo, reverb, EQ, pitch and speed, undo and redo.
  • Quick Recorder: one big button, keeps recording across tabs, timer, auto-stop, WAV, WebM or MP3, past recordings to play, download, or send to the Mixer or Editor.
  • Loops and Keys: drum, bass and keys loops made by the app, on-screen piano with four sounds.
  • Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded and nothing costs extra. Loudness is an approximate meter.

MP3 export uses the free lamejs encoder (LGPL), included with the app.
