Write a clear report about your child for an occupational therapist, doctor, NDIS planner, support coordinator, school or similar. Pick a template, answer the prompts, then edit the wording before you copy or print it.

Privacy Everything you type stays on this device. Drafts are saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Another device, or clearing site data, will not have these drafts. Initials are enough if you would rather not store a full name.

Disclaimer This is a writing aid for parents and carers. It is not medical, clinical, educational or legal advice, and it does not replace an assessment or letter from a qualified professional.
