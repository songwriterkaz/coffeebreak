Coffeebreak Fox Hunt

Radio direction finding in the bushland. Follow the loudest signal and find the hidden foxes.

Keyboard and mouse, touch screens and VR headsets are all supported.

How to play

Hidden radio transmitters, called foxes, are somewhere in the park. Each one beeps its morse ID every few seconds.

Your Yagi antenna is directional. Turn to face different ways: the signal is loudest and clearest when you point straight at a fox. Static means you are far away or pointing wrong.

Walk toward the loudest signal. When the meter is pinned near full, turn up the attenuator to cut the strength so you can still tell which way is loudest.

When you are close, press Find (F key, or trigger in VR). Level 1 has one fox, level 2 has three, level 3 has five. Be quick, the timer is running.

Level 3 has a time limit of six minutes. Your score drops the longer each fox takes, and your best times and best score are saved on this device.

Desktop: WASD or arrows to walk, drag mouse to aim, Q/E change attenuator. Mobile: joystick to walk, drag to aim, slider for attenuator. VR: press Enter VR on the title screen, thumbstick to walk, point the antenna in your first controller, read the receiver on your other hand, trigger to find, grip button to raise the attenuator.
