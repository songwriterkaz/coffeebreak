Coffeebreak Helping Hands

The two person mime acting game

How to play

  1. Storyteller stands in front and tucks their arms behind their back.
  2. Hands stands right behind and pushes their arms through, under the Storyteller's arms.
  3. Pick a story. The Storyteller talks. The Hands does every hand move, without seeing.
  4. The Hands can get a secret surprise card to sneak in.
  5. When time is up, the audience gives stars. Then swap.
