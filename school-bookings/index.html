About Coffeebreak School Bookings

Coffeebreak School Bookings helps schools, parents and carers. Book parent-teacher meetings with live availability, report absences, and sign excursion permission slips. Staff get a day dashboard with meetings and absences, a smart waitlist and a coffee order queue. Everything runs in your web browser and is saved on your own device.

Coffee cart: order a coffee, see your cart total and press Buy it now. Pay by PayID (Teachers Mutual Bank): darkokaz@gmx.com, or pay at the counter. This app takes no card payments.
