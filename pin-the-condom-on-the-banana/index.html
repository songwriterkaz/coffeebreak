Pin the Condom on the Banana

Ages 14+ · Sex education game

A Coffeebreak party game with a safe-sex twist. Drag or tap to pin the condom right on the tip of the moving banana. The closer you get, the more points you score.

  • Each round: 5 condoms and 20 seconds.
  • Drag the condom from the tray and let go over the banana tip, or just tap where you want it.
  • Perfect = 100, Great = 70, Good = 40. Reach the target score to go up a level.
  • From level 4 the room goes dark, just like a blindfold.

    How to use a condom: quick tips

      Condoms help protect against sexually transmitted infections (STIs) and pregnancy. Free or low-cost condoms are available at many sexual health clinics. If you have questions, talk to a trusted adult, a school nurse, a GP or a sexual health clinic.
