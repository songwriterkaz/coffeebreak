This look book brings together the Coffeebreak fashion designs: made to order wigs in pink, blonde, lilac and pastel rainbow, soft leather glasses with a lens slot, tattoo flash that works as prints, and plush pals from the Coffeebreak TV series.

Each look is styled around the Coffeebreak colours: espresso, latte, cream and every shade of pink.
