Coffeebreak Blockbuster Tools

Cauldron spell, report burner, shredder and sponsors. Just for fun, all on your device.

You're Fired spell

Type a name, pick your ingredients and drop it in the cauldron. This is a pretend spell for letting off steam. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody is contacted.

Report burner

Paste a report you don't like, or load a text file or picture, then watch it burn in digital flames. Your file never leaves this device and the real file is not changed.

Shredder

Type or load the stuff you don't like and feed it to the shredder.

Sponsors

Show the sponsors who support Coffeebreak Blockbuster. Add a sponsor with their name, link, logo and tier. Sponsors are saved on this device, and you can export them to share or back up.

About

Coffeebreak Blockbuster Tools is a set of just-for-fun stress relief tools from Coffeebreak, made in Newcastle NSW, to go with Coffeebreak Blockbuster.

  • Cauldron spell: give someone a custom name and drop it in the bubbling cauldron for a pretend "you're fired" spell.
  • Report burner: burn reports you don't like in digital flames.
  • Shredder: shred stuff you don't like into strips or confetti.
  • Sponsors: show off the sponsors who back your movies.

Everything works offline and stays on your device. The spell is pretend and does not affect anyone's real job. Please be kind to real people.
