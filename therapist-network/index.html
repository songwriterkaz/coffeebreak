Apply to join the provider list

Psychologists, counsellors, psychotherapists, social workers, OTs and other registered or credentialed practitioners can apply. The application fee is $50 AUD.

Karen's team checks your registration with the body you name (for example AHPRA, ACA, PACFA or AASW) before any approval. Your listing only goes live once it is approved. The fee covers the review and is payable whether or not the application is approved.

About

The Coffeebreak Therapist Network is a provider list put together by Karen O'Brien, a Coffeebreak songwriter and creator from Newcastle NSW. Therapists apply, Karen's team checks their registration, and approved providers appear in the public directory so clients can find someone who suits them.
