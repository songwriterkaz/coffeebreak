How bidding works

  1. Choose a lot and enter a bid above the current highest bid. Your bid opens an email to with your details. A bid counts once it is received and confirmed.
  2. Current highest bids and bid history are updated by Coffeebreak after each confirmed bid.
  3. If your bid is the highest when the countdown ends and it meets the reserve, you win. Or pay the buy-now price to win straight away.
  4. Winners pay by PayID to using the lot code and your name as the reference, for example CB-FLASH-01 SMITH.
  5. After payment you receive the full resolution flash file, a certificate of authenticity as your 1 of 1 digital edition, emailed to you.

Prices are in AUD. Buying a digital artwork gives you personal ownership of that file and certificate. Copyright stays with the artist unless agreed in writing.

Digital edition (NFT style collectible, 1 of 1)

Every flash design is sold as a single digital edition, 1 of 1. The winner or buy-now buyer pays by PayID to darkokaz@gmx.com with the lot code and their surname as the reference. After payment is confirmed, Coffeebreak emails the buyer the full-resolution flash design file and a numbered 1 of 1 certificate of authenticity. This is a digital collectible delivered by email. It is not recorded on a blockchain.
