DESCRIPTION
Coffeebreak Espresso Music Maker is a whole music studio that fits on your phone. Play, record, edit and mix songs with no sign in, no ads and no outside service.
What is inside:
- Multitrack studio with instrument, drum and audio tracks, undo and redo, and WAV export
- Nine instruments: piano, guitar, strings, synth, 808, organ, bass, bells and your own sampler
- Piano roll, drum machine and an on screen keyboard, with MIDI keyboard support where your browser allows it
- Record notes with a count in, record audio from the microphone, import audio files and edit clips
- Mixer with volume, pan, reverb, echo and tone for every track
- Looper, sampler, tuner, metronome, chord helper, rhyme finder and sheet music you can print
- A built in Song Maker: type lyrics, pick a style and mood, and it builds a backing track and melody into your studio
Projects save on your device. Works offline once loaded.
